# Open questions to validate with Namtarn

Bring this to the discovery sessions in weeks 1 to 2. Each answer replaces an *(assumption)* in [01-business-plan.md](01-business-plan.md).

## Baseline

- [ ] Cases in the last 12 months: how many, of which types, at which offices, at what fee each?
- [ ] Hours spent per case, split into: client back-and-forth, document assembly, travel and waiting, rework after a rejection.
- [ ] How many leads per month does the Facebook page produce, and how many become paying clients?
- [ ] What has she spent on ads, and what came back?
- [x] City: Chiang Mai, confirmed. Still open: does she also handle clients in other provinces remotely, or only cases filed at Chiang Mai Immigration?

## Case mix and process

- [ ] Her top 3 case types by volume and by margin (they may differ). These become the MVP playbooks.
- [ ] For each: the exact document list she uses, the forms, the order the office wants them in, the fees, the typical timeline.
- [ ] Which steps require the applicant in person, and which she can do alone or with an authorisation letter?
- [ ] Which offices accept online booking, e-Extension, online 90-day reports and online TM.30, and which insist on walk-ins?
- [ ] What does a "wasted trip" look like: the five most common reasons a submission bounced in the last year?

## Knowledge

- [ ] Where do her templates live today (Word files, PDFs, phone photos, LINE chats)?
- [ ] Which cover letters or explanation letters does she reuse, and which wording has worked with specific officers?
- [ ] Rules that differ between offices that a newcomer would not know.
- [ ] Rule changes she has seen in the last 12 months and how she found out.

## People and money

- [ ] Senior-agent draw versus base salary: what does she want, and what monthly income does she need to commit full time?
- [ ] Does she know a candidate for the first validator or runner role?
- [ ] Is she comfortable with a junior staff member submitting on her behalf, or must she be present at the office for relationship reasons?
- [ ] Which languages do her clients speak? Which she cannot serve today?

## Legal and trust

- [ ] Does she operate as an individual today, or through a company? Does she invoice?
- [ ] Has she ever been asked to "help" with bank balances, and how does she refuse?
- [ ] Is she willing to sign a public no-fraud policy and to turn clients away over it?
- [ ] Any officer or office relationships that constrain how we scale (for example, she is known personally at one desk)?

## Product

- [ ] Would she use a web dashboard, or does everything have to work from her phone and LINE?
- [ ] Would her clients upload documents to a portal, or must intake work through LINE and Messenger?
- [ ] What would make her trust a pre-filled form enough to sign off on it in five minutes?
